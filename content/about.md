<span class="marker marker--split">No. 01<span class="marker-rule"></span>About Bodhya</span>
<h1>Bihar has the talent. We're building the <em>bridge</em>.</h1>
<p class="page-hero-lead">Bodhya emerged from a simple observation: Bihar has talented students, but they lack access to tech opportunities and networks that students in metros take for granted.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="band--tint">
<div class="shell">
<div class="section-head">
<span class="marker">No. 02</span>
<h2 class="section-title">Who we bring together</h2>
<p class="section-lead">Bodhya is a community-driven employment initiative. The work is convening three groups that rarely meet in Bihar, then turning that into jobs for students.</p>
</div>
<div class="card-grid">
<div>
<span class="card-eyebrow">Open source</span>
<h3>FOSS communities</h3>
<p>Bodhya is built in the open, on open source values — no gatekeeping, no geography. Contributing is how students get seen.</p>
</div>
<div>
<span class="card-eyebrow">Industry</span>
<h3>Mentors and employers</h3>
<p>Professionals across India who volunteer their time — reviewing work, answering questions, and opening doors to internships and jobs.</p>
</div>
<div>
<span class="card-eyebrow">Bihar</span>
<h3>Colleges and educators</h3>
<p>Teachers and campuses across the state who host the workshops, run the clubs, and reach students where they already are.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>
<section class="band">
<div class="shell">
<div class="section-head">
<span class="marker">No. 03</span>
<h2 class="section-title">The story</h2>
</div>
<div class="about-story">
<div class="about-story-copy">
<p>Bodhya was created as a collective response to that gap. It brings together FOSS communities, industry professionals, and local educators to help students in Bihar build visibility, work on real-world open source projects, and access internships and jobs without having to leave home.</p>
<p>Starting January 2026, Bodhya is being built full-time as a community initiative — supported by <a href="https://fossunited.org" class="link-body" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">FOSS United's</a> ecosystem, volunteer mentors from across India, and students who are already proving that world-class tech talent exists in Bihar when given the right opportunities.</p>
<p class="about-story-note">We will soon list the names of active volunteers here.</p>
</div>
<div class="about-story-aside">
<div class="name-specimen">
<span class="name-specimen-label">The Name</span>
<span class="name-specimen-word">Bodhya</span>
<span class="name-specimen-lang">Sanskrit</span>
<p>Means “worthy of knowledge” or “capable of being awakened.” It reflects our belief that every student has potential — they just need the right spark and opportunity.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
