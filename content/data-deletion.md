This page explains how a learner, a parent or legal guardian, or a facilitator can ask us to delete a Bodhya Learn account and everything stored with it. You do not need to install the app to make this request.

<div class="doc-callout">

**Never signed in? There is nothing to ask us for.** Bodhya Learn can be played with no account at all, in the app and in the browser. Played that way it never sends us the learner's name, her answers or her progress — all of it stays on her own device, and we hold nothing about her to delete. To erase what is on the device, open **☰ → My progress → Start fresh** in the app. Please also read [Data on the learner's own device](#device) below, which explains Google backup.

<span lang="hi">**अगर सीखने वाली ने कभी साइन-इन नहीं किया, तो हमसे कुछ माँगने की ज़रूरत नहीं।** बिना खाते के खेलने पर उसका नाम, उसके जवाब और उसकी प्रगति — कुछ भी हम तक नहीं आता, सब उसी डिवाइस पर रहता है। डिवाइस से मिटाने के लिए ऐप में **☰ → मेरी प्रगति → नए सिरे से शुरू करो** दबाएँ। नीचे *Google बैकअप* वाली बात ज़रूर पढ़ें।</span>

</div>

## हिन्दी में — खाता और जानकारी कैसे हटवाएँ

**कौन कह सकता है?** सीखने वाली ख़ुद, उसके माता-पिता या अभिभावक, या उसकी फैसिलिटेटर। इसके लिए ऐप दोबारा इंस्टॉल करने की ज़रूरत नहीं है। आप पूरी बात हिन्दी में लिख सकते हैं।

**क्या करें?** [vishal@bodhya.net](mailto:vishal@bodhya.net) पर ईमेल भेजें, विषय में लिखें **“Delete my data”**, और इतना बताएँ —

1. सीखने वाली का **चुना हुआ नाम**, जैसा ऐप में दिखता है;
2. **कैंपस, कक्षा या फैसिलिटेटर का नाम** (अगर वह किसी क्लास से जुड़ी है);
3. या फिर वह **अभिभावक का मोबाइल नंबर**, जिस पर खाता बनाते समय WhatsApp कोड आया था;
4. आप कौन हैं — ख़ुद सीखने वाली, या उसके अभिभावक/फैसिलिटेटर।

हम कोई सरकारी पहचान-पत्र या काग़ज़ नहीं मांगते। खाता ढूँढने के लिए चुना हुआ नाम, या वही अभिभावक का नंबर, काफ़ी है।

**क्या-क्या हटेगा?** उसका खाता और उससे जुड़ी सारी जानकारी — नाम और अवतार, पाठ की प्रगति (सितारे, सिक्के, जेम्स), क्विज़ और टेस्ट के जवाब, फैसिलिटेटर को भेजे गए सवाल और उनके जवाब, हाज़िरी, मूल्यांकन, **अभिभावक का वह मोबाइल नंबर और सहमति का रिकॉर्ड** जिस पर खाता बना था, और **बोली की सारी रिकॉर्डिंग — ऑडियो फ़ाइलें भी** (बोली सुविधा अगस्त 2026 में हटा दी गई थी; उसके बाद ऐप माइक्रोफ़ोन नहीं माँगता)। बच्चे की आवाज़ की कोई कतरन हम नहीं रखते।

**कितना समय लगेगा?** **7 दिन** के अंदर हम आपकी बात मिलने की पुष्टि करेंगे, और खाता पक्का होने के **30 दिन** के अंदर जानकारी हटा देंगे। हो जाने पर हम आपको लिखकर बताएँगे।

**सिर्फ़ कुछ हटवाना है?** सब कुछ हटाना ज़रूरी नहीं। आप कह सकते हैं कि सिर्फ़ रिकॉर्डिंग हटा दें, या सिर्फ़ भेजे गए सवाल हटा दें, और बाक़ी पढ़ाई की प्रगति रहने दें। ईमेल में बता दें कि क्या हटाना है।

## How to request deletion

Send an email to [vishal@bodhya.net](mailto:vishal@bodhya.net) with the subject **“Delete my data”**, and include:

- the learner’s **chosen name** as it appears in the app;
- the **campus, class or facilitator name**, if the learner joined through a classroom;
- **or** the parent’s or guardian’s **mobile number** that the account was created with — the number the WhatsApp code was sent to;
- whether you are the learner, or the parent/guardian or facilitator making the request for a child.

We do not ask for any government ID or document. We only need enough detail to find the right account and to be reasonably sure the request comes from the learner or a responsible adult — because wrongly deleting a child’s work is as harmful as wrongly keeping it.

<div class="doc-callout">

**How accounts are identified.** An account is created with a **parent’s or guardian’s own mobile number**, confirmed by a one-time code sent to it on WhatsApp — so either the learner’s chosen name and campus, or that number, is enough for us to find it. We never collect an email address, and we never collect a **learner’s** own phone number, so we cannot look an account up by either of those.

**If the learner never signed in, there is nothing for us to delete.** Bodhya Learn can be played with no account, entirely offline, in the app or in the browser. In that case nothing was ever sent to our server — the progress exists only on that one device, and clearing it there (below) removes everything. You do not need to write to us at all.

<span lang="hi">**अगर सीखने वाली ने कभी साइन-इन नहीं किया,** तो हमारे पास कुछ भी नहीं है — पूरी प्रगति सिर्फ़ उसी डिवाइस पर है। नीचे बताए तरीक़े से डिवाइस साफ़ कर दें, बस।</span>

## What gets deleted

On request we permanently delete the learner’s account and all of the information linked to it:

<div class="doc-tablewrap" tabindex="0">

| Data | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Student record | The chosen name, avatar, class/grade band, campus and cohort, and the campus sign-in secret. |
| Guardian’s mobile number | The parent’s, guardian’s, or facilitator’s number the account was created with, and any record of a code sent to it. |
| Consent record | The record of the permission the account rests on: which adult agreed, when, by which route, and the exact wording they agreed to. |
| Lesson progress | Stars, coins, gems, completed activities, and lesson attempts. |
| Quiz and test answers | Module test attempts, answers given, and scores. |
| Doubts and replies | Questions the learner sent to a facilitator and the replies received. |
| Attendance | Attendance days and device pings recorded while the learner was using the app. |
| Learning events | Activity-level records such as wrong answers and time spent, used to help facilitators teach. |
| Evaluations and milestones | Facilitator evaluations and any milestone or belt gates cleared. |
| Voice recordings *(withdrawn feature)* | The Bhojpuri dialect feature was withdrawn in August 2026 and the app has had no microphone access since. If the learner contributed to it before then, deletion removes any recordings and transcriptions together with the speaker record linking them to her. |
| Device identifier | The random per-install identifier used for attendance. |

## Data on the learner’s own device {#device}

Bodhya Learn is offline-first, so most information sits on the device itself — and for a learner who has never signed in, **everything** does. Clearing that copy never requires contacting us — but on Android please read the note about **Google backup** below, because uninstalling on its own no longer removes every copy. How you do it depends on how she plays:

<div class="doc-tablewrap" tabindex="0">

| How she plays | How to clear the device copy |
| --- | --- |
| **The Android app** (Bodhya Learn from Google Play) | In the app, open **☰ → My progress → Start fresh** and confirm. This erases every star and gem on the phone immediately, and it is the one method a learner can use unaided — it needs no reading, no internet and no help from us. **Uninstalling is no longer enough on its own.** If the phone’s Google backup is switched on, Android keeps a copy of this app’s saved progress in the *device owner’s own Google account* and restores it if the app is ever installed again. That copy belongs to the phone’s owner, not to us: we cannot see it, read it or delete it. To be rid of it, use **Start fresh** *before* uninstalling, so the emptied state replaces the stored copy at the next backup; or delete the backup yourself in the Google Drive app under *Backups*. Clearing the app’s storage (*Settings → Apps → Bodhya Learn → Storage → Clear data*) also empties the device copy. |
| **In a web browser** at [learn.bodhya.net/play](https://learn.bodhya.net/play) | Clear the site data for `learn.bodhya.net` in the browser: in Chrome, *Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → View permissions and data stored across sites → learn.bodhya.net → Delete data*. Note that a shared computer keeps a separate copy for each browser profile, so clear it in the one she uses. |

</div>

Clearing the device copy does **not** by itself delete anything already synced to our server — use the email request above for that.

## What we keep, and for how long

- **Encrypted backups.** Deleted records may survive for a short while in our routine encrypted server backups, until those backups expire on their normal rotation and are destroyed with it. We never restore a deleted account from a backup.
- **Voice recordings are destroyed, not anonymised.** The Bhojpuri dialect corpus was **withdrawn in August 2026**, and no version of Bodhya Learn has requested microphone access since. If the learner contributed to it before then, deletion removes the speaker record, every transcription and verification, and the **stored audio files themselves**. We do not keep a stripped-down copy of a child’s voice.
- **Spent sign-in codes.** A one-time WhatsApp code is only ever stored as a hash, never in readable form and never in any log. Spent and expired codes are pruned automatically **30 days** after they are used or expire, whether or not an account is deleted.
- **Google backup (Android only), which is not ours.** The Android app’s on-device progress is included in Android’s standard Auto Backup, so a copy may sit in the *device owner’s own Google account* and come back if the app is reinstalled. We do this so that a child who reinstalls the app — especially one playing with no account — does not lose everything she has learned. It is held by Google for the phone’s owner under [Google’s own privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we have no access to it and cannot delete it for you. It never contains a guardian’s mobile number. See the Android row above for how to clear it.
- **Nothing else.** We do not retain a copy for advertising, profiling, or sale. We have no advertising IDs and no third-party analytics.

## How long it takes

1. We **acknowledge** your request within **7 days**.
2. We **complete** the deletion within **30 days** of confirming the account.
3. We **write back** to confirm when it is done.

## Deleting only some information

You do not have to delete everything. You may instead ask us to correct a name or the mobile number on file, remove only the voice recordings, or remove only the doubts and messages, and keep the rest of the learning progress. Just say what you want removed in the email — including anything not listed above.
