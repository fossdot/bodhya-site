## How Complaints Are Handled

1. **Raise it.** Write to the Grievance Officer at the email above with a description of the issue, and — for a payment complaint — the transaction ID, date, and amount.
2. **Acknowledgement.** We will acknowledge your complaint within **5 business days** of receiving it.
3. **Resolution.** We will investigate and communicate an outcome within **30 days** of receiving the complaint, as required under the Information Technology Rules.

### If our resolution does not satisfy you

You are not limited to us. You may escalate independently:

- **Personal data complaints** — approach the **Data Protection Board of India** under the DPDPA 2023.
- **Payment complaints** — raise the matter with **Razorpay**, the payment processor, through their grievance channel, or with your own bank or card issuer.
- **Consumer complaints** — approach the appropriate **Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission**, or the National Consumer Helpline (1915).
