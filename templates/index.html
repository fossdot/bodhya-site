No. 03

Why Bodhya?

The tech ecosystem in India is growing rapidly. For too long, most opportunities flowed towards metros and IITs while overlooking the immense potential within Bihar. Students from tier-2 and tier-3 colleges, especially in Bihar, rarely get a fair shot. Talent is universal, but opportunity is not. Bodhya exists to build the bridge.

Since January 2026, Bodhya has worked to change that: connecting students with mentors, running workshops in the state, and opening routes into paid work.
