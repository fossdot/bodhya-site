<div class="section-head">
<span class="marker">No. 03</span>
<h2 class="section-title">How complaints are handled</h2>
</div>
<div class="steps-grid">
<div>
<span class="step-index">01</span>
<h3>Raise it</h3>
<p>Write to the Grievance Officer at the email above with a description of the issue, and — for a payment complaint — the transaction ID, date, and amount.</p>
</div>
<div>
<span class="step-index">02</span>
<h3>Acknowledgement</h3>
<p>We will acknowledge your complaint within <strong>5 business days</strong> of receiving it.</p>
</div>
<div>
<span class="step-index">03</span>
<h3>Resolution</h3>
<p>We will investigate and communicate an outcome within <strong>30 days</strong> of receiving the complaint, as required under the Information Technology Rules.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="escalation">
<h3>If our resolution does not satisfy you</h3>
<p>You are not limited to us. You may escalate independently:</p>
<div class="escalation-routes">
<div>
<span class="escalation-label">Personal data</span>
<span class="escalation-copy">Approach the <strong>Data Protection Board of India</strong> under the DPDPA 2023.</span>
</div>
<div>
<span class="escalation-label">Payments</span>
<span class="escalation-copy">Raise the matter with <strong>Razorpay</strong>, the payment processor, through their grievance channel, or with your own bank or card issuer.</span>
</div>
<div>
<span class="escalation-label">Consumer</span>
<span class="escalation-copy">Approach the appropriate <strong>Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission</strong>, or the National Consumer Helpline (1915).</span>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
