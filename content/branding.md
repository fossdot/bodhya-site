<div class="section-head">
<span class="marker">No. 05</span>
<h2 class="section-title">A few rules</h2>
</div>
<div class="brand-rules">
<div>
<h3>Please do</h3>
<ul>
<li>Leave clear space around the mark, at least the height of the B on every side</li>
<li>Use the SVG wherever the medium allows it — it stays sharp at any size</li>
<li>Pick the variant that suits the ground: terracotta or ink on light, cream on dark</li>
<li>Write the name as “Bodhya”, in sentence case, with no full stop</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div>
<h3>Please don't</h3>
<ul>
<li>Stretch, rotate, crop, or add effects to the mark</li>
<li>Recolour it outside the three variants above</li>
<li>Place the terracotta mark on a dark or busy ground — use the cream one</li>
<li>Rebuild the wordmark by setting “Bodhya” in a font</li>
</ul>
</div>
